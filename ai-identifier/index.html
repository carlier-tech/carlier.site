<!-- WHY AI IDENTIFIER -->

Less guessing. More knowing.

A photo is the whole input.

No model numbers, no forms, no research tabs. Frame the object and let the model read the shape, surface, markings, and patina. It works on the worn, the dirty, and the things you can't quite name.

Value comes as a range, not a promise.

Every result includes an estimated price band and a confidence score. It's a starting point for research — not a dealer offer, a grading opinion, or a guarantee of what someone will pay.

Your collection stays yours.

Finds, photos, notes, and API keys live on your device. The scan image goes to Google Gemini only to produce the identification. There is no central shelf of your collection.

<!-- FAQ -->

Before you scan.

Is the value in the app what I can sell the object for?

No. It's an estimated range for information only — not a dealer offer, a professional appraisal, or a guarantee of what eBay or a shop will pay. Condition, markings, provenance, and demand can change the real price a lot.

Does AI Identifier work on iPhone?

Not yet. AI Identifier is Android-only on Google Play. If you'd like to be notified about iOS, send us feedback.

Do I need a Google API key?

Identification uses Google Gemini. In Settings you can add a free key from Google AI Studio. The key stays on your device, and Google's quotas and terms apply. A key is optional if the app is configured with its own access.

What happens to my photos and collection?

The scan photo is sent to Google Gemini for identification. Your collection, photos, notes, and keys stay on your phone. There is no Carlier account or central collection server. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do the free scans work?

Download the app and scan up to three objects with identification and a value estimate, no card required. Premium unlocks unlimited scans and the full collection. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email feedback@carlier.site, or follow the tips in the feedback guide so we can reproduce the issue quickly.
