The short version

The Site collects almost nothing.

carlier.site is a static informational site. No accounts, no contact forms, no advertising, no analytics trackers, and no cookies. A language preference can live in your own browser — and it never reaches us.

Your app data stays on your device.

By default, saved items, history, and settings stay local. We do not run a central profile of your collections. If you contact us, we only receive what you choose to send.

Third parties are named.

Where an app uses a third-party AI service such as Google's Gemini API, the relevant input is sent to that provider under its own policy. App stores and Google Fonts are the other outside services involved.
