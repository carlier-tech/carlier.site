Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you visit this website (carlier.site, the “Site”) and when you use our mobile applications (the “Apps”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and making it clear how your data is handled.

privacy_policy.html Applies to the Site and the Apps Last updated October 2026

1. The Website

The Site is a static informational website that presents our products. It is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

  • No accounts, forms, or trackers: The Site has no sign-up, no contact form, and no advertising or analytics trackers. We do not use cookies.
  • Language preference: If you switch the Site into another language, that choice is stored in your own browser (via localStorage) so the Site can remember it. This preference stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Hosting logs: Like most websites, the server that hosts the Site may temporarily record standard technical details (such as your IP address, browser, and the page requested) for security and operational purposes. We do not combine this with identifying information.
  • Store links: The Site links to Google Play and other app stores. Once you follow such a link, that store's privacy policy applies.

2. The Apps

Our Apps process information differently depending on the app, and each App has its own, more specific Privacy Policy. In general:

  • Local storage: By default, your data within an App (such as saved items, history, or settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate centralized servers that hold your personal data or your collections.
  • Third-party AI services: Some Apps use third-party artificial-intelligence services (such as Google's Gemini API) to deliver their core feature. Where an App does this, the relevant input is transmitted to that provider for analysis, and that provider's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • API keys: If an App lets you enter your own API key, that key is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to it.
  • Feedback and support: If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to send (usually your email address and your message).

Please refer to the Privacy Policy published on each App's store listing for the details that apply to that specific App.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and the Apps.
  • To provide the features an App is built around (which may include sending input to a third-party AI service for analysis).
  • To respond to support requests and to communicate security or update notices.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local by default: Data an App stores about your usage stays on your device unless an App's own policy says otherwise.
  • No central profile: We do not keep a database of your personal data or collections for the Site.
  • Security: While we take reasonable safeguards, no system is perfectly secure. For Apps, we recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

5. Third-Party Services

The Site and the Apps rely on a small number of third parties:

  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): Where an App uses Gemini, input is sent to Google's APIs. Your use of that feature is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • App stores and fonts: The Site is distributed through app stores and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process standard technical data under their own policies.

6. Global Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: You can ask us what information we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You can ask us to delete information you have given us (such as a support email). For data stored locally in an App, you can exercise this yourself by clearing the App's data or deleting the App.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can simply stop using the Site or an App; the Site itself sets no tracking cookies to opt out of.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site and the Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions such as the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page, and the “Last Updated” date above will reflect the latest revision. You are advised to review this page periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@carlier.site.
